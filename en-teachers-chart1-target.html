The 150,000-teacher competition: announced target vs actual appointments

~61,000 appointed out of 150,000 targeted — less than half the goal after five batches

“Actual appointments from the start of the competition through the fifth batch do not exceed 60,000 to 62,000 teachers out of the 150,000 targeted.”
Total competition target 150,000
Approved by PM Madbouly for appointment (Jan 2025) 72,000
Actually appointed through the 5th batch ~61,000

After five batches, actual appointments did not exceed 41% of the announced target — while 28,600 posts were excluded from the fifth batch alone.
